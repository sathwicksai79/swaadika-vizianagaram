Our Mission

Every city has its own food culture, and Vizianagaram is no exception. From morning tiffin centres to family restaurants and evening snack stalls, the city has many local food spots worth discovering.

SWAADIKA aims to make finding these places easier. Our goal is to bring local food spots together in one platform, helping people explore different places, discover popular dishes and make informed food choices.

We also aim to give local eateries more online visibility and help people discover the variety of food available in their city.

🌐

Understanding the Platform

SWAADIKA is a local food discovery platform focused on Vizianagaram. It organizes food spots into three categories based on everyday food preferences:

Morning Tiffins: Explore places serving idli, dosa, medu vada, upma and other breakfast favourites.
Restaurants & Dining: Discover restaurants offering meals, biryani, curries and different cuisines.
Evening Snacks: Find places serving punugulu, mirchi bajji, pakoda, samosas and other popular snacks.

Each listing provides available shop information, price details, popular vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, and location details wherever available.

⭐

Understanding Our Ratings

Choosing a food spot can sometimes be difficult. SWAADIKA includes a Machine Learning-based rating prediction feature to provide users with an additional reference when exploring restaurants.

  • Customer Rating: The available customer rating and review count associated with a restaurant.
  • SWAADIKA Prediction: An estimated rating generated using a trained Random Forest Regression model based on the information and features available in our dataset.
  • Restaurant Comparison: Compare two restaurants side by side to understand differences in their ratings, prices, cuisines and other available information.

Note: SWAADIKA predictions are machine learning estimates based on available dataset patterns. They are not actual customer reviews or guaranteed ratings and should be considered an additional reference when exploring food spots.
