Our Mission

Every city has its own food culture, and Vizianagaram is no exception. From morning tiffin centres to family restaurants and evening snack stalls, the city has many local food spots worth discovering.

SWAADIKA brings local food spots together in one clean platform, helping you explore different eateries, discover popular dishes, and make informed choices.

We aim to give local culinary gems greater online visibility while helping food lovers discover what tastes right.

🌐

Understanding the Platform

SWAADIKA organizes Vizianagaram food spots into three intuitive categories:

Morning Tiffins: Explore places serving idli, dosa, medu vada, upma, and breakfast favourites.
Restaurants & Dining: Discover dining spots offering meals, biryani, curries, and multi-cuisine platters.
Evening Snacks: Find spots serving punugulu, mirchi bajji, pakoda, samosas, and fast food quick bites.

Each listing provides price ranges, popular vegetarian/non-vegetarian dishes, digital menus, and exact locations.

⭐

Understanding Our Ratings

Choosing where to eat shouldn't be guesswork. SWAADIKA includes Machine Learning-based rating prediction as an additional data-backed reference.

  • Customer Rating: Historical ratings and review counts from patrons.
  • SWAADIKA Prediction: ML estimates generated via trained Random Forest Regressors based on price, volume, and menu diversity.
  • Side-by-Side Comparison: Compare two spots directly to see price, ratings, cuisines, and specials side by side.

Note: Predictions are statistical ML estimates designed as helpful guidance alongside actual reviews.
